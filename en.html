// FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Cyber really free?

Yes, every feature is free. No subscription, no premium tier.

Which permissions does Cyber need?

Only what its features use (manage messages, roles and channels, kick and ban…), never Administrator. You can remove a permission at any time: only the feature that needs it will stop.

Does Cyber speak English?

Yes. Commands, replies and buttons are available in English and French. Choose French, English, or both with /config language or in the dashboard: in mixed mode, Cyber writes in English only in the channels or categories you pick. Each member can override it with /language. Command names are in English for everyone. The Academy lessons and the dashboard are still in French for now.

What data does Cyber store?

Only what it needs: your server settings, members' XP and warnings. Nothing is sold or shared. Any member can see or delete their data with /mydata. Details are in the privacy policy (in French).

Who made Cyber?

Cyber is built by Enzo, a French networking and IT student passionate about cybersecurity.
